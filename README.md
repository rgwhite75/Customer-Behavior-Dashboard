# Customer Behavior Dashboard

## Business Problem

A leading retail company is experiencing shifts in customer purchasing behavior across demographics, product categories, and sales channels. To improve sales performance, customer satisfaction, and long‑term loyalty, the company needs to identify the key factors — such as discounts, reviews, seasonal trends, and payment preferences — that influence buying decisions and repeat purchases.

## Dataset
This project uses a dataset containing customer behavior information.

Contants:
Total Records: 3,900
Columns: 18
Missing values: A total of 37 records had missing data

## Exploratory Data Analysis
Before building the dashboard, I performed the following to gain a better understanding of the dataset:
* Checking for missing values
* Reviewing data types
* Renaming columns for readability
* Exploring relationships between variables
* Creating visualisations to identify potential patterns

## Key takeaways 

* Seasonal patterns strongly influence purchases
  * Customers buy different product types depending on the season.

* High review ratings correlate with higher purchase amounts
  * Many high-value purchases have strong review scores.

* Younger customers tend to buy lower-cost items
